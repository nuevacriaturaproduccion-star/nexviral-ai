NexViral AI

NEX Publicador is a desktop tool that helps creators prepare, schedule and publish their own short videos to their own social accounts (TikTok, YouTube and Facebook Pages) from one place.

What it does

  • Cuts long videos into short clips, adds subtitles and covers.
  • Writes titles, descriptions and hashtags for each network.
  • Lets the creator review every post and publish it now or schedule it.

TikTok integration

The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit). Before each post, the “Post to TikTok” screen shows the account nickname, a preview, an editable title, privacy options, interaction settings, commercial content disclosure and TikTok’s Music Usage Confirmation. Nothing is posted until the creator confirms.
