Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Who we are

NexViral AI / NEX Publicador (“the App”) is a desktop tool that runs locally on the user’s computer.

2. Data we access

  • TikTok: with your permission (Login Kit), basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) via user.info.basic, and the ability to upload/publish videos you choose via video.upload and video.publish.
  • YouTube / Facebook: the permissions you grant to publish to channels and Pages you manage.
  • The videos, titles and descriptions you prepare in the App.

3. How we use it

Only to show which account will receive a post and to upload and publish the content you confirm. We do not sell data, show ads, or use your data for any other purpose.

4. Storage

Access tokens, settings and videos are stored locally on your own computer. They are not sent to our servers. Content is sent only to the platform you publish to (e.g. TikTok). Text generation features may send titles/descriptions to an AI provider configured by the user.

5. Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties, except the platforms you choose to publish to.

6. Your choices

You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time in the App (Canales) or in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and permissions. Deleting the App folder removes all locally stored data.

7. Children

The App is not intended for users under 18.
